How to Take Care of Cats

💧 Fresh Water

Provide clean drinking water every day.

🧹 Clean Environment

Keep the litter box and sleeping area clean.

🪮 Grooming

Brush your cat's fur according to its coat type.

🧸 Playtime

Give your cat toys and time for daily activities.

🩺 Health

Arrange regular veterinary check-ups.

🏡 Safe Home

Provide a comfortable place where your cat can rest.
