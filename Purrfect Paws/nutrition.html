Healthy Eating for Cats

🥩 Protein

Cats need animal-based nutrients as part of a balanced diet.

🥣 Balanced Food

Choose complete cat food suitable for your cat's age.

💧 Hydration

Always provide access to fresh drinking water.

⚖️ Proper Portions

Follow feeding guidelines and monitor your cat's body condition.

🚫 Unsafe Foods

Avoid chocolate, onions, garlic and alcohol.

🩺 Veterinary Advice

Ask a veterinarian about special dietary requirements.
